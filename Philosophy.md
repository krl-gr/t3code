# Philosophy

The philosophy behind this fork is to focus entirely on a pure agentic interface experience. We aren't trying to build the "one app to rule them all" for coding or anything else.

I view Up.Computer as the missing puzzle piece that seamlessly connects with your other apps.

In practice, this means we don't plan to add project trees, build out complex code-review flows, or integrate an internal browser inside Up.Computer. Instead, we are focusing on making integrations with truly great IDEs, external terminals, real browsers, and other specialized apps as smooth as possible.

When you click to open a Markdown file, you aren't trapped in a clunky, built-in editor — Up.Computer instantly launches it in your favorite Markdown app. Ready to review code? We route it straight to your preferred diff tool or a beautiful IDE like Zed. Need to browse the web? Up.Computer will fire up a real Chrome browser.

Our ultimate priorities are genuine agentic interaction, full user context control, a seamless experience, and flawless integrations.
